# PDFDrop privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

PDFDrop edits and converts PDF files **entirely on your PC**.

- **No data leaves your computer.** PDFDrop does not upload, send or share your documents or any information about them.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.**
- **What is stored:** your settings, the list of recently opened PDFs and, if you choose "Remember this signature", your drawn signature, in `%APPDATA%\PDFDrop` on your PC. Delete that folder to remove them.
- **Microsoft Store version:** to check your trial or purchase, Windows contacts the Microsoft Store. That is handled by Windows under [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); PDFDrop receives only whether the app is licensed.

Questions: https://github.com/tiger1247/pdfdrop/issues
